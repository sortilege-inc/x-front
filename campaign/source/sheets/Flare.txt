Famous: The character is widely
known—at least among a certain
group of people or a population.
They may be well-liked or they may
be hated, but either way, they are
famous. They have an edge when
making an Ego check to persuade
someone who thinks favorably of
them. They have trouble when
making an Ego check to persuade
someone who dislikes them.
Gullible: The character is easy to
fool. People lying to them have an
edge on their Ego checks to
persuade the character of
something.
Honest: The character is a terrible
liar. They have trouble any time
they make an Ego check that
involves telling a lie. However, they
have an edge on any friendly Ego
(persuasion) checks in which
they’re truthful.
Hounded: The
character is a
member of a group of
people who are
persecuted for simply
being themselves.
X-Gene: The
character has mutant
genes that cause
them to develop
super-powers.
Young: The character
is a minor, under 18
years old. If possible,
they must report in to
their parents or
guardians on a
regular basis.
Wisdom (Permanent): The character can
use their Ego defense score against Logic
attacks too.
Flight (Permanent): The character can fly.
Their combat Flight Speed is equal to
their rank times their Run Speed. Outside
of combat, they can move three times
their Flight Speed.
Elemental Control (Electricity/Energy)
===============
Elemental Burst (Standard, Instant,
Range: 10 spaces): The character makes
a ranged attack against an enemy in line
of sight. If the attack is a success, it
inflicts regular damage. On a Fantastic
success, the enemy takes double damage
instead and is stunned for one round.
Elemental Blast (Standard, Instant,
Range: 10 spaces, Cost: 5+ Focus): The
character makes a ranged attack with an
edge at an enemy in line of sight. For this
attack, add +1 to the character’s Agility
damage bonus for every 2 points of Focus
they spend. On a success, an affected
target takes that total damage. On a
Fantastic success, an affected target
takes double that total damage and is
stunned for one round.
Elemental Barrage (Standard,
Instant, Cost: 15 Focus, only
Electricity): The character
designates a space within their line
of sight. The attack can affect
every enemy within 10 spaces of
that. The character makes a single
Ego check and compares it to
each target’s Resilience defense.
Affected enemies take half regular
damage. On a Fantastic success,
they take full regular damage and
are stunned for one round.
Elemental Push (Standard, Instant,
Cost: 10 Focus): The character
makes an Ego attack against the
target’s Agility defense. If the
attack succeeds, the character can
move the target in any direction,
up to 1 space times the character’s
rank. On a Fantastic success, the
target also takes regular damage,
is knocked prone and is stunned
for one round.

fails an action check, they gain
an edge on the check if they try
the same action again on their
next turn.
Signature Attack (Supernova):
The character is known for
favoring a particular kind of
weapon or attack power. They
have an edge when making
attacks that way.
Small: The character’s size is
small, which adds +1 to their
Melee and Agility defenses and
takes –1 from their Run Speed.

controversial but seemingly theoretical. When

by one of the cheering students seemingly
erupting in light & knocking out the power for
three blocks, an investigation occurred, and
state officials came to the home of Taylor

handed her over despite protests. It garnered
national media coverage.
Current Age: 15
Energetic, impatient, tactless. Very ADHD, very
inexperienced.

with her family and friends means pretending
that she isn’t a mutant.

Elemental Control
==============
Energy Absorption (Reaction,
Instant, Cost: 15 Focus): [Trigger:
The character takes Health
damage.] The character can take
any Health damage done to them
(after applying any damage
reduction), ignore it and add that
number to their Focus instead. In
this way, they can increase their
Focus up to double their regular
maximum Focus. Once the
combat is over, any extra Focus
over the character’s regular
maximum Focus score fades
away.
This power cannot be used again
until any Focus the character
gained in this way is spent.
Elemental Ricochet (Electricity
only, Standard, Instant, Range:
40 spaces, Cost: 10 Focus):
The character makes a ranged
attack against a target in line of
sight. If the attack is a success,
it inflicts regular damage. On a
Fantastic success, energy also
bounces off the first target to
another in line of sight, and the
character can make a new
attack against that target,
adding the new range to the
previous attack’s range. This
can be repeated until an attack
is not fantastic.
Supernova (Standard, Instant,
Cost: 15+ Focus): The character
makes an Ego check and
compares that against the
Resilience defense of every
enemy within 10 spaces. For
these attacks, add +1 to the
character’s Ego damage bonus
for every 2 points of Focus they
spend. On a success, an
affected target takes half that
total damage. On a Fantastic
success, an affected target
takes full damage and is
stunned for one round.
