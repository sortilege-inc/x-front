Combat Trickery (Reaction,
Instant, Cost: 5 Focus): Once per
battle, when the character is
attacking targets of equal or
higher rank, the character
automatically rolls a 1 on their

character is attacking multiple
targets, all the targets must be of
equal or higher rank.
Inspiration (Standard, 1 Round):
The character inspires an ally in
earshot. The ally gains an edge
on all action checks until the start
of the character’s next turn.
Uncanny (Permanent): Any
damage multiplier for attacks
against the character’s Focus is
reduced by 1.

to touch the target. On a

Concentration, Cost: 5 Focus
per turn, Range: 10 spaces):
The character picks a foe in
their line of sight. That foe has
trouble on all action checks
made for the power’s duration.
There is no limit on the range
between the character and the
foe once the power is activated.

character gains an edge on all
action checks made while this power
is in effect. The character must pay

working.

Met Toya at a mutant pride march that went

Current age: 17
Full of spirit and self-sure.
Goal: Tabitha doesn’t see a future for herself
worth having, except if it’s a means to the end
of other mutants also having a future. She’s
going to fight hard and go down fighting.
Barrier: Tabitha has broken off from those she
felt limited her, but that also deprives her of
institutional and social support. The future she
wants to build, she can’t build alone, but she
almost compulsively burns every bridge she
crosses.
