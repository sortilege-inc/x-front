---
title: X-FRONT
subtitle: A Marvel Multiverse campaign · young mutants who take direct action
---

Toya Terrence went to the mutant pride march in Tallahassee meaning to stay incognito. When things
started getting rough and the police wouldn’t protect the marchers, Toya stepped up. Tabitha Fanning,
who had walked out of Xavier’s academy, met Toya there, and the two of them formed the seeds of
X-FRONT: mutants who have stopped waiting for protest and petition to work.

Hermes Montoya came out of a conversion therapy camp in the Arizona desert with X-FRONT’s help. Mohamed
Salem left home in Houston before he could be kicked out, and was part of that rescue. Cody Kennedy is
out of jail and in violation of parole. In Florida, a new law lets the state take mutant children from
their homes, and after a high-school basketball game went dark for three blocks, it took Taylor
Crenshaw.
