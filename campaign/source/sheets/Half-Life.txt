Elemental Control (Energy/Force)
=========================
Elemental Burst (Standard,
Instant, Range: 10 spaces): The
character makes a ranged attack
against an enemy in line of sight.
If the attack is a success, it inflicts
regular damage. On a Fantastic
success, the enemy takes double
damage instead and becomes
Blinded for 1 round.
Elemental Blast (Standard,
Instant, Cost: 5+ Focus, Range:
10 spaces): The character makes
a ranged attack with an edge at
an enemy in line of sight. For this
attack, add +1 to the character’s
Agility damage bonus for every 2
points of Focus they spend. On a
success, an affected target takes
that total damage. On a Fantastic
success, an affected target takes
double that total damage and has
trouble on all actions for one
round.
